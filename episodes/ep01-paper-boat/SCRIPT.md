# HasthiPeel · Episode 1: "The Paper Boat"

**Runtime:** ~5:00 · **Master:** 16:9 · **Audience:** ages 2–6 and their families · **Made for Kids:** yes
**Setting:** a little village by the sea: red-earth lane, Mangalore-tile roofs, banana plants, monsoon rain
**Lesson (never said as a moral, only shown):** *gentle is a kind of strong.*

**Cast**
- **HasthiPeel** — the little elephant. **Non-verbal**: giggles, gasps, "ooh", "hmm?", sighs, and his
  signature **tiny trumpet "toot"** when he's happy. Acts with his face, ears and trunk.
- **The Frog** — small, green, unbothered. Non-verbal: "ribbit", and a throat-puff. *(Name to be
  decided. A Konkani name would suit him.)*
- **Narrator** — warm, unhurried, smiling voice. The only one who speaks words.

> **Narrator note:** please confirm how **HasthiPeel** is pronounced (stress and vowel lengths) before
> recording. It's the brand name and must sound the same in every episode.

---

## Script

Line IDs (**N01…**) are for recording: one audio file per line, named `N01.wav`, `N02.wav`, …
Rig cues are in `code font`. ★ marks a Short candidate.

### COLD OPEN · 0:00–0:12 — *motion from frame one, no narration*
**S01 · wide, slow push-in.** Heavy rain on the lane. The house door bursts open and HasthiPeel
runs out, a paper boat held high in his trunk, ears flapping.
`walk · expression: joy · trunk raised holding prop`
SFX: rain (heavy), door bang, squelchy footsteps.

**S02 · medium.** He skids on the wet earth, *whoa*, and sits down in a puddle with a **SPLASH**.
Beat. The boat is still held safely above his head, perfectly dry. He looks up at it… and **giggles**.
`slip: tilt back + squash → sit · expression: surprised → joy`
SFX: skid, big splash. HP: "whoa!", giggle.
★ **Short #1 "Safe!"** (S01–S02 + title sting, ~17 s)

### TITLE STING · 0:12–0:17
Jingle (3 s, same every episode). Title card: **HasthiPeel**, then *The Paper Boat*.

### SETUP · 0:17–0:55
**S03 · wide establishing, slow pan.** The lane in the rain: puddles, roofs, banana leaves dripping.
- **N01** — "By the sea, in a little village with red-earth lanes… lives a little elephant."
- **N02** — "His name means 'elephant's calf'…"

**S04 · close.** HasthiPeel turns to camera, delighted to be introduced.
- **N03** — "He is… HasthiPeel!"
HP: **toot!** `expression: joy · ear flap`

**S05 · medium.** He holds the paper boat up proudly.
- **N04** — "Today, it is raining. Pitter-patter, pitter-patter."
- **N05** — "And when it rains, HasthiPeel knows exactly what to do…"
- **N06** — "…make a paper boat!"

**S06 · wide, the reveal.** He turns. Across the lane is the biggest puddle he has ever seen.
- **N07** — "And there it is. The biggest puddle in the whole lane."
HP: "Ooooh!" `expression: surprised → joy`

### TRY 1, too big · 0:55–1:40
**S07 · medium.** He wades *into* the puddle to put his boat down.
- **N08** — "HasthiPeel steps into the puddle, to put his boat on the water…"

**S08 · wide.** His big foot sends out a **wave**. The boat bobs, tips, and flips upside-down.
SFX: big slosh, little *plip*.
- **N09** — "Oh dear. When you're an elephant… even your tiptoes make waves."
HP: sheepish little "hmm". `expression: sad (mild) · ears down`

**S09 · close.** He fishes the boat out with his trunk and shakes it. *Drip, drip.*
- **N10** — "Never mind. He tries again."

### TRY 2, too strong · 1:40–2:40 ★
**S10 · medium.** This time he stays on the edge and lowers the boat gently with his trunk tip.
- **N11** — "This time, HasthiPeel stays on the edge. Gently… gently… there!"

**S11 · wide, hold.** The boat sits on the water. And sits. It doesn't move at all.
- **N12** — "But the boat doesn't go anywhere."
HP: "Hmm?" `head tilt`

- **N13** — "Boats need wind. And who has the best blower in the whole village?"

**S12 · close.** HasthiPeel slowly looks down at his own trunk. Cross-eyed realisation. Big grin.
`look: cross-eyed at trunk · expression: joy`
- **N14** — "HasthiPeel does!"

**S13 · medium.** He takes an ENORMOUS breath, swelling up rounder and rounder… and BLOWS.
`blow: inhale (body inflates) → trunk forward · cheeks puff`
SFX: long inhale, huge WHOOOOSH.

**S14 · wide.** The boat shoots into the sky, spinning, spinning…

**S15 · medium, hold.** …and lands, *plop*, right on top of his head. Like a hat.
HP looks up at it, cross-eyed again… and giggles.
- **N15** — "Hmm. That was a little bit… too much wind."
★ **Short #2 "Too Much Wind!"** (S10–S15, ~40 s). The strongest gag in the episode.

### TRY 3, still too strong · 2:40–3:20
**S16 · medium.** He sets the boat down again and blows *a bit less*. Still WHOOSH. The boat
zooms across the puddle, bumps the far edge, tips over… and slowly goes soggy.
- **N16** — "HasthiPeel tries a smaller blow. Whoosh! Still too big!"
- **N17** — "Now the paper boat is all soggy. It sinks… down… down… down."
SFX: whoosh, bump, soft *gloop*.

**S17 · close, slow push-in.** HasthiPeel in the rain. Ears drooping, trunk hanging. Rain drips off
his ears. Music turns soft.
`expression: sad · ears down · no blink for a long hold`
- **N18** — "Poor HasthiPeel. Sometimes, being big makes small things very tricky."
*(Let this land. 3 full seconds with no narration.)*

### THE TURN · 3:20–4:05
**S18 · close on the puddle.** *Ribbit!* A little green frog hops onto a fallen banana leaf floating
on the puddle.
- **N19** — "Ribbit! Who's this?"
- **N20** — "It's a little frog… sitting on a banana leaf."

**S19 · medium.** Raindrops bounce off the leaf. It floats perfectly.
- **N21** — "Banana leaves don't go soggy in the rain. They float!"
HP: "Ooh!" `expression: surprised`

**S20 · close.** The frog puffs out its throat, a tiny *puff*, and the leaf drifts forward, just a little.
- **N22** — "And look how the frog moves it… just one tiny puff."

**S21 · close.** HasthiPeel's eyes light up. He picks up another banana leaf and sets it on the water.
Then he brings his trunk close, closes his eyes in concentration… and does the smallest, gentlest *phoo*.
`expression: sleepy (eyes-closed concentration) · blow (small)`
- **N23** — "So HasthiPeel tries the smallest, gentlest puff he can…"
SFX: a tiny "phoo".

### RESOLUTION · 4:05–4:35
**S22 · wide, slow track.** The banana-leaf boat glides across the puddle, the frog riding on it
like a captain. HasthiPeel walks alongside, giving tiny puffs. The frog ribbits happily.
- **N24** — "…and the banana-leaf boat sails across the puddle! With a frog for a captain!"
HP: **toot, toot!** `walk · expression: joy`

**S23 · medium.**
- **N25** — "Sometimes, the biggest trunk only needs… the smallest puff."

### SIGN-OFF · 4:35–5:00
**S24 · wide → medium.** The rain softens. Evening. Warm lamplight spills from the doorway. The frog
hops off home. HasthiPeel turns to the camera.
- **N26** — "And that's the end of today's little story."
`turn to camera · wave`
- **N27** — "Bye-bye, HasthiPeel!"
HP: **toot!** ★ **Short #3 "Captain Frog"** (S22–S24, ~35 s)

> Made-for-Kids videos can't carry end screens or cards, so there's no 20-second end-screen tail.
> The episode ends on the wave.

## Numbers
- **27 narration lines, ~230 words.** At an unhurried kids' pace (~115 words/min), about 2:00 of
  speech in a 5:00 episode. The other ~3 minutes is action, sound and silence, which suits this
  age. The gags need room.
- **24 shots.** Average ~12 s per shot, slow by grown-up standards, about right for preschool.
